About Sign Base

Established in 2009, Sign Base is a trusted signage and branding company in Botswana.

We specialise in designing, manufacturing and installing high-quality signage, corporate fascia, vehicle branding, stickers, printed apparel and number plates.

Our story

With over 17 years of experience, we help businesses of all sizes stand out and make a lasting impression, from a single shopfront to multi-tenant centre signage.

Our commitment

Innovative design, exceptional quality and reliable service on every job.

Mission and values

Mission: to help businesses communicate and be remembered through well-made branding.
Values: quality, creativity, professionalism and reliability.
